One request, end to end

Follow one request through the stack

A customer disputes an invoice. Watch the request move from the channel to the system of record, and see which layer does what.

  1. Channels and signals. The dispute arrives by email.
  2. Human command. The charter and the do-not-automate list set the limits.
  3. Orchestration. The workflow engine starts the dispute flow.
  4. Agents. The finance agent investigates.
  5. Intelligence. A GPT-class model reads the contract; retrieval grounds it.
  6. Harness. Policy limits, the decision threshold and verification check the proposal.
  7. Tools and integration. A released interface commits the credit.
  8. Data and context. The system of record and the ledger tie out.
  9. Assurance. The trace, evals and audit keep the evidence.
