Bring your own trace

The trace format

Use JSON Lines: one event per line. Three fields are required: t, agent and type. The rest are optional. Add a run field with two values, such as baseline and fault-injected, to compare two runs.

FieldMeaningExample
tTime of the event, ISO 8601. Required.2026-11-02T08:03:12Z
agentWho acted: an agent, a person, the harness or a system. Required.credit
typeregister, receive, handoff, check, propose, escalate, approve, reject, commit, block, reverse or demote. Required.propose
runWhich run the event belongs to.baseline
caseThe case or correlation ID that joins a thread.C-1008
to, from, targetThe other party of a handoff, or the agent whose action is reversed or demoted.supervisor
role, levelOn register: agent, person, control plane or system, and its autonomy level.agent, 3
control, resultThe control that fired, and pass or fail.DQ-01, fail
routeOn commit: auto (inside limits) or person (after approval).auto
amount, confThe value at stake and the agent’s confidence.1200, 0.87
msg, evidenceWhat happened, and the evidence a reviewer sees.Proof of delivery attached.

How the headline numbers are counted

  • Actions. Proposals made by agents.
  • Auto-committed. Commits with route auto: inside limits, no person needed.
  • Routed to a person. Escalations to a decision card.
  • Blocked by a control. Actions a control stopped outright.
  • Reversals. Committed actions later undone. Each one is a labeled error.
  • Dropped handoffs. Handoffs sent but never received by the other agent in the same case.

The sample trace is illustrative and not client data. Files you load are read in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded or stored. Related: the ten-part harness, control TRC-01 what to trace and which dashboards to read and questions leaders ask.
