Limits and what is real

Real. The graphs, the routing, the traversal and every check are code. The answer is one call to a GPT-class model, with a smaller model as fallback.

Not run. Other model families appear on the map so you see the choice. The demo labels them “routed to, not run in this demo” and shows no output for them.

Limits. Eight questions per visitor per hour. The site shares a daily allowance of model calls that resets at midnight UTC. Your questions are not stored.
