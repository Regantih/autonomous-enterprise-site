The rules it applies

How the designer decides

  1. Default ceiling. Materiality and reversibility set the start point, from up to L4 for low and easily reversible to L0–L1 for high and irreversible. See the table.
  2. Data quality. If the input data fails its gate, the step is capped at L2.
  3. Regulation. A regulated step or key financial control is capped at L2 until legal and audit approve more.
  4. Evidence. A step runs no higher than the level it has proven.
  5. External counterparty. A commitment to a third party lowers the ceiling one level for the first 90 days.
  6. Weakest link. A step cannot run above the handoff it depends on. The chain runs at its lowest step or handoff.
  7. Decision threshold. The agent acts alone only when confidence is at least L ÷ (G + L).
  8. Controls. The designer pulls every control that the step level, the model family and the step context require from the library.

Thresholds are illustrative. Set your own with internal audit.
