How this page was built

  • Every agent, task, level, limit and owner comes from the book. Each one cites its chapter and section.
  • Where the book names a task but not its level, the page says “Level not stated”. It does not guess.
  • Where the book names few agents, the page shows only those. Tax and trade has four because the book names four.
  • Product names from the book’s platform examples are left out. Meridian is the book’s worked-example company.
  • Thresholds are the book’s illustrative values. Each company sets its own.
